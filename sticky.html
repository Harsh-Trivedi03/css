My Header

On Scroll Sticky Header (Inside Section)

The header will stick to the top when you scroll down, but only within this section.

Scroll back up, and the sticky effect will be removed, returning the header to its normal position.

Here's how the sticky position works:

1. The page initially displays the header in its normal flow, and it scrolls with the rest of the content.

2. As you scroll, the header stays at the top of the section, thanks to the position: sticky; CSS property.

3. The header will stop being sticky when you scroll past this section.

Importance and use cases:

- Sticky headers inside sections are useful for keeping content-specific navigation visible.

- They are ideal for multi-section pages where you want the header to only stay within its section.

- This effect can be applied to other elements like menus, banners, or toolbars within a specific part of the page.
